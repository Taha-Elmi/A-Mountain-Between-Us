# The words of the Outro comic, in Persian (fa, the default) and English (en). It plays once
# Farhad is through the mountain, and then the game returns to the main menu.
# One block per page, in the order the pages play; the name in brackets is the image it shows
# ((black) for a black page), and "fade S" makes the page take S seconds to come in. The lines
# of a page appear one per click, in the order written here.
#
# Each line starts with who says it in [brackets], then how its box looks and where it sits:
#   caption        a square box (narration, or Farhad telling the story); [Narration] is one anyway
#   speech         a rounded bubble (everyone else, unless told caption)
#   oval           an elliptical bubble
#   plain          the words alone, light on the dark, no box
#   at X Y         the box's centre, in percent of the page from its top-left corner
#   width W        the widest the box may grow, in percent of the page's width
#   tail X Y       a bubble's tail points here (percent of the page, as above)
# then its text in both languages; another fa: or en: row starts a new row of the text.
# Farhad > Preview Comic Words draws every page with its words into ComicPreviews/ to check the
# layout.
#
# Speakers:
#   [Narration]  نریشن
#   [Messenger]  فرستاده قصر
#   [Farhad]     فرهاد
